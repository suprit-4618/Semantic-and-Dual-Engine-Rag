The James Webb Space Telescope is the largest optical telescope in space and can view objects too old, distant, or faint for the Hubble Space Telescope.

A black hole is a region of spacetime where gravity is so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape.

The Perseverance rover landed on Mars in 2021 to search for signs of ancient microbial life and collect rock and soil samples.

The Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy that contains our Solar System and is estimated to contain 100 to 400 billion stars.

Neutron stars are the collapsed cores of massive supergiant stars and are the smallest and densest currently known stellar objects.

The Apollo 11 mission was the spaceflight that first landed humans on the Moon on July 20, 1969.
